For teachers

Students aim with the pull distance and launch angle, then answer the level's prediction before Launch unlocks. Most answers depend on each student's own pull distance, so neighbors' answers won't match. Wrong answers get targeted feedback (forgot the ½, forgot the square root, used g = 9.8, wrote 0.8 instead of 80%). After two misses a "Show solution" link appears; using it gives up that level's prediction star.

Stars: one for predicting without the solution, one for clearing the fort, one for clearing it within the par number of pebbles. Stars are saved in each student's own browser.

Physics model: g = 10 m/s² and no air resistance in flight, so KE + GPE stays constant while the pebble flies. All losses happen in the band (its efficiency). Heights are measured to the pebble's center from the ground, or from the valley floor in Level 7. Blocks break when the pebble hits them with enough kinetic energy (glass 15 J, wood 40 J, stone 150 J).

Discussion prompts:

  • Change only the launch angle. Does the KE at launch change? Why not?
  • At the top of an angled shot, why isn't the KE zero? (Use the replay slider to find the peak.)
  • In Level 8 both pebbles get the same KE. Why does the heavy one leave so much slower?
  • Where does the "lost" energy in the band go? Is it destroyed?

The Sandbox (after Level 8) lets you set any force, mass and efficiency for demos. It shows a live preview of the launch energy while you aim.
