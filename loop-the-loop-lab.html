Key moments

    Energy

    PE is zero at the bottom of the loop. Thermal energy comes from the collision and, if it is on, from friction. The dashed line is the bullet's starting kinetic energy.

    Momentum

    Right and up are positive. After the collision the bullet rides in the block, so its momentum is counted in Block. The track pushes on the block around the loop, so momentum changes there.
