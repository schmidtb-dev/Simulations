Model notes

  • u is the velocity of the mass entering or leaving, measured in the ground frame. For the rocket, u = v − urel. The mass-transfer term (u − v)·dm/dt is the thrust for the rocket and the drag of the incoming sand for the car.
  • Motion is one-dimensional: positive is to the right, or up for the launch. Sand falls from the chute with horizontal velocity u; its vertical motion is not part of the model. The track is frictionless and there is no air resistance.
  • Burn rate and exhaust speed are constant. The energy ledger counts the exhaust's kinetic (and gravitational potential) energy at the moment it leaves; the fuel releases ½·|dm/dt|·urel² every second.
  • Each run is computed in advance with a fourth-order Runge–Kutta integrator (8000 steps) and agrees with the analytic solutions to better than 0.01%. Each vector type keeps one scale for the whole run, so arrow lengths can be compared over time. Click or drag on any graph to jump to that moment.
